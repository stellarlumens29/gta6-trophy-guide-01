Read this first — the missable list

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy launch-week guides flag as potentially missable. Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 is gone when the chapter ends, and there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus to get it back. Do it in Chapter 1 (after Down to Earth, follow Lucia to the balcony in the morning) and keep a Chapter 1 manual save. Strangers & Freaks has one time-sensitive spot: the Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 shows an hourglass icon — race it the moment it appears and save first.

Save rule for the whole run: a manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one untouched save from just before the final mission. 15 manual slots plus one autosave.
